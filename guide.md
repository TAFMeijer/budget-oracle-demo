#### What this is

A demo that answers questions about the Global Fund's **published grant budgets** in plain
English. The data is public: "Grant Budgets – Reference Rate" from the
[Global Fund Data Service](https://data-service.theglobalfund.org/downloads).

#### What you can ask about

**Where** country · region  **What** component · module → intervention
**How** cost category · cost group  **When** funding cycle (2017-2019, 2020-2022, 2023-2025) · year

#### Worth knowing

- These are **budgets** in US$, not spending or disbursements.
- No cycle named means the current one, 2023-2025. Say "all cycles" to see everything.
- If a question could mean two things, you get a question back with options to pick from.
- Module totals for 2017-2019 and 2020-2022 undercount: the public data leaves part of those
  budgets without a module name. Country, component and cost totals are complete.
- A personal demo, not an official Global Fund product. Check numbers before quoting them.
