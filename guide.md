#### What this is

A demo that answers questions about the Global Fund's **published grant budgets** in plain
English. The data is public: "Grant Budgets – Reference Rate" from the
[Global Fund Data Service](https://data-service.theglobalfund.org/downloads).

#### What you can ask about

**Where** country · continent · sub-continent · Global Fund region (WCA, HIA1, HIA2, MENASEA, EECA, LAC, Asia)
**What** component · module → intervention
**How** investment landscape (level 1 → level 2) · cost category
**Who** principal recipient · PR type · lead implementer
**When** grant cycle (5, 6, 7) · year

#### Worth knowing

- These are **budgets** in US$, not spending or disbursements.
- No cycle named means the current one, Grant Cycle 7. Say "all cycles" to see everything.
- Costs come at three levels; there are no cost inputs in the published data.
- If a question could mean two things, you get a question back with options to pick from.
- Module totals for Grant Cycles 5 and 6 undercount: the published data leaves part of those
  budgets without a module name. Country, grant and cost totals are complete.
- A personal demo, not an official Global Fund product. Check numbers before quoting them.
